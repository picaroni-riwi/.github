## Our mission

Picaroni is a foundation that cares about penguins. Its mission is to protect penguins and the ocean ecosystems they depend on, through habitat protection, research, rescue, and public awareness.

## What we do

- Habitat protection
- Research
- Rescue
- Public awareness
